# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Internal administrators, government staff, and potentially public viewers (for published reports). They are focused on accuracy, efficient data entry, and reviewing regional science and technology (S&T) gender and development (GAD) metrics.

## Product Purpose
To manage, aggregate, and publish yearly Gender and Development (GAD) reports for DOST Region IX. It allows authorized personnel to draft, review, and finalize reports before they are publicly accessible.

## Brand Personality
Authoritative, transparent, precise, structured. It must feel like a premium, high-trust government digital tool, avoiding the "bureaucratic clunkiness" of older systems.

## Anti-references
- Classic "Gov" generic Bootstrap templates
- Start-up/SaaS "playful" aesthetics (no excessive gradients, no cartoonish illustrations, no bouncy animations)
- Overwhelming data-dump interfaces (data should breathe)
- "AI Purple/Blue" default aesthetic (as explicitly banned by the rules)

## Design Principles
1. **Clarity over Density**: Data must be easy to scan and read.
2. **Authoritative Restraint**: Use high-contrast neutrals and typography to build trust, rather than relying on heavy colors.
3. **Hardware Accelerated Flow**: The interface should respond instantly, with fluid micro-interactions confirming user intent.
4. **Asymmetric Precision**: Break the mold of symmetric, centered cards. Use space intelligently to separate content groups.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast text-to-background ratio (WCAG AA).
- Clear, descriptive labels for all form inputs.
- Easily clickable touch targets for mobile accessibility.
